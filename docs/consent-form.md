## Purpose
We are recording classroom lectures to develop and evaluate automatic speech recognition (ASR) and topic modeling systems for educational applications. Your lecture audio will be used to:
- Train and test ASR models on real classroom acoustics
- Develop topic segmentation and clustering algorithms
- Create study materials (notes, flashcards) from lecture content

## What You Will Do
- Allow us to record your lecture using a microphone placed in the room
- Recording duration: approximately 45–90 minutes
- No action required from you beyond normal teaching

## Data Collected
- **Audio recording** of the lecture (Opus format, 48kHz mono)
- **Metadata:** Room, date, device position, subject name (no student names)
- **No video, no student identities, no biometric data**

## Data Storage & Security
- Audio stored in encrypted MinIO bucket on local GPU server
- Access limited to research team (2 people)
- Audio automatically deleted **30 days after session processing completes**
- Metadata retained for research validity (anonymized)
- No cloud upload, no third-party access

## Your Rights
- **Voluntary participation:** You may decline or withdraw at any time without consequence
- **Data deletion:** Request deletion of your recording at any time
- **No identification:** Recordings never linked to your identity in publications
- **Questions:** Contact [Your Email] for any concerns
